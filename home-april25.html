Seller Objections Answered

The questions every seller actually asks.

What is included in the $17,000 fixed fee? ▼

Professional photography, full MLS syndication, pricing strategy, prep guidance, marketing, showings coordination, negotiation, escrow management, and closing. One fixed fee. No percentage. No surprise charges.

What happens if my home does not sell? ▼

You owe nothing for the fixed fee portion of the listing. The listing agreement terms govern your specific obligations, and we walk through them with you before you sign. No hidden cancellation traps.

Do I still have to pay a buyer's agent? ▼

After the NAR settlement, sellers are no longer required to offer buyer-agent compensation through MLS. You decide case-by-case whether to offer any, and how much. We advise on what is working in your neighborhood right now. You stay in control.

Who represents me at closing? ▼

We do. Every negotiation. Every contingency. Every counter. One client per deal means nobody in our office is representing the buyer. Your side. Only.

Do you work in all Santa Clarita Valley cities? ▼

Yes. Valencia, Saugus, Canyon Country, Newhall, Stevenson Ranch, Castaic, Acton, and Agua Dulce. 27 years covering the entire valley. Every neighborhood has its own rhythm and we know them.

What if I want to sell to a buyer I already know? ▼

We handle unrepresented buyers cleanly. You get full seller-side representation. The buyer negotiates directly with us. Same $17,000 fixed fee. No dual agency. No blurred lines.

Are real estate commissions really negotiable? ▼

Yes. California Business and Professions Code Section 10140.6 requires disclosure that all commissions are negotiable. The $17,000 fixed fee is our published rate. The conversation is free.

Why would I not use a percentage-based listing agent? ▼

The work to sell an $800,000 home is the same as selling a $400,000 home. Same photos. Same negotiations. Same escrow. The percentage model charges the owner of the more valuable home twice as much for identical work. A fixed fee aligns what you pay with what actually happens.
