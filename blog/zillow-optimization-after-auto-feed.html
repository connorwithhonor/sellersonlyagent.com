Zillow Optimization: What to Adjust After the Auto-Feed Pulls In

Connor MacIvor·May 2026·6 min read

Zillow receives roughly 90% of all online real estate traffic in Southern California. Buyers, agents, neighbors, and curious parties all start there. A Santa Clarita listing that performs well on Zillow drives the downstream funnel. A listing that performs poorly on Zillow rarely recovers anywhere else. The auto-feed gets the listing onto the platform; the optimization pass determines whether it actually performs there.

What the auto-feed delivers and what it does not

The MLS feed populates Zillow with the core data — price, address, square footage, beds, baths, public remarks, photo gallery. Zillow generates its Zestimate, applies its own inferred amenity tags, and renders the listing in its standard template. What it does not do without agent intervention:

  • Curate the photo order for maximum scroll-through
  • Verify all amenity tags are populated
  • Add video content via Zillow's direct video upload
  • Flag price reductions, open houses, or virtual tour availability
  • Connect the listing to the agent's Zillow professional profile for credibility signals
  • Reply to buyer inquiries that arrive through Zillow's contact form

All of these require agent action. Most listings never get them.

The Zillow polish checklist

Photo gallery

  • Reorder so the strongest 5 photos sit in positions 1-5
  • Verify lead photo is the strongest exterior (twilight or golden-hour front)
  • Confirm photo count matches MLS (no missing images)
  • Verify photos render correctly on mobile (where most Zillow traffic happens)
  • Remove any low-quality or duplicate shots

Listing details

  • Confirm Zillow's beds/baths/sqft match MLS exactly
  • Verify lot size displays correctly
  • Check year built accuracy
  • Review HOA fee and frequency
  • Confirm school district assignment is correct

Amenity tags

  • Pool, spa, view, fireplace, garage spaces, parking type
  • Heating and cooling type
  • Flooring types per room (Zillow's facts table)
  • Appliances included
  • Special features (smart home, solar, EV charger, etc.)

Description rendering

  • Confirm the MLS description shows in full
  • Check that line breaks render correctly
  • Verify no truncation at a critical sentence
  • Confirm fair housing compliance
  • Spot-check for any portal-specific filtering or scrubbing

Video and tours

  • Upload walkthrough video directly to Zillow's video field
  • Embed 3D virtual tour if produced (Matterport or similar)
  • Add drone footage if available
  • Confirm video plays correctly on mobile and desktop

Events and flags

  • Add upcoming open house dates with full details
  • Confirm "New Listing" flag is active
  • Flag price reductions when applicable (drives "Recent Price Cut" alerts)
  • Verify the listing is set to actively accept showing requests
  • Check virtual tour availability flag

The agent profile credibility layer

Zillow listings claimed and verified by an active agent profile rank higher in default search results. Connor's listings are claimed within minutes of activation, with the agent profile fully populated (headshot, bio, license number, years of experience, customer reviews). This is a credibility signal Zillow uses to differentiate listings — and it costs nothing to capture.

Reviewing the Zestimate context

Zillow displays the Zestimate prominently on every listing. If the list price is well above the Zestimate, buyers may anchor to the lower number. If well below, buyers may assume there is something wrong with the property.

The Zestimate is not the pricing strategy — the CMA is. But the Zestimate visibility on Zillow is a context factor every Santa Clarita seller should be aware of, and Connor reviews it for every listing before launch. In a small number of cases the Zestimate is meaningfully off (typically because the algorithm missed recent upgrades), and Zillow allows agents to add context that helps inform buyers.

Inbound inquiry routing

Every Zillow inquiry routes through Connor's AI Voice Agent and his direct line within minutes. The inquiry form is checked daily; unanswered inquiries cost leads. Auto-responses confirm receipt while Connor or the AI follows up substantively.

Ongoing maintenance during the listing

The Zillow optimization is not a one-time event. Throughout the listing period, the listing gets:

  • Weekly photo refresh if any update applies (newly added open house, completed touch-up)
  • Daily inquiry response checks
  • Price-reduction announcements timed to Thursday for maximum re-attention
  • Open house events refreshed each weekend
  • Final status update when the listing moves to pending or closed
"Zillow is not a passive distribution channel. It is an active marketing platform with ranking signals, agent claim mechanics, and dozens of fields most listings never populate. The difference between a fully optimized Zillow listing and an auto-fed one is the difference between 8,000 weekly views and 1,200." — Connor MacIvor

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Zillow do automatically?
Pulls price, address, beds/baths, public remarks, and photos from MLS. Generates Zestimate, populates inferred amenity tags, renders in standard template. Agent-side adjustments differentiate strong from weak.
Can agents change Zillow details?
Yes, within limits. Agents reorder photos, confirm amenity tags, add video, manage events, reply to inquiries. Core data flows from MLS.
Does Zillow ranking affect visibility?
Yes. Zillow uses completeness, photo quality, agent claim, recency, and click-through to rank. Fully optimized listings rank higher in default sort.
How often should Zillow be checked?
Weekly minimum, daily during the 7-day launch window. Status, price, and amenity changes reflected immediately.
